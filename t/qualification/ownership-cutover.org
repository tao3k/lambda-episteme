#+title: Lambda Episteme ownership cutover

:PROPERTIES:
:ID:         lambda-episteme-ownership-cutover
:STATUS:     current
:DATE:       2026-09-17
:END:

* Boundary

Lambda Episteme owns vertical knowledge, ontology Profiles, Scenarios, Cases
and their source qualification.  Its public module roots are =decision-kind=,
=diataxis=, =healthcare= and =ontology=.

ADR, GitOps and software-lifecycle assurance moved to Lambda Aitia.  Their
sources, tests, profiles, scenarios, tools and historical qualification
receipts are not retained here under aliases or versioned compatibility
namespaces.

* Acceptance

The cutover is accepted only when the public build, the four remaining module
test lanes and the source-collection contract pass against a parent registry
pinned to the same child revision.  A focused pass against a dirty or stale
parent registry is diagnostic evidence only.

The canonical =just test= entry is the native Gerbil/ASP suite.  The former
Wendao, RDF, SQL-policy and Python-alignment surfaces are absent rather than
retained behind a compatibility lane.  ADR policy, Source, fixer and dedicated
fixture ownership is also absent here; Lambda Aitia is the sole contributor
owner for those semantics.
